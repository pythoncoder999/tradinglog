💡 Essential Day Trading Tips (Easy to Remember)

🎯 Rule of 2%

Never risk more than 2% of your total capital on a single trade. This keeps you in the game even after losses.

⏰ Time is Money

Best trading hours: 9:30-11:30 AM and 2:00-4:00 PM EST. Avoid lunch hour (12-2 PM) - low volume!

📊 Follow the Trend

"The trend is your friend until it ends." Don't fight the market direction - ride with it.

🛑 Stop Loss Always

Set stop losses BEFORE entering trades. Stick to them no matter what. Emotions kill profits.

📰 News = Volatility

Check economic calendar daily. FOMC, earnings, GDP - these move markets. Trade the reaction, not the news.

🧘 Psychology Wins

Stay calm, stick to your plan. FOMO and revenge trading destroy accounts. Take breaks after losses.
